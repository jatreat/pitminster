About this site

Friends of Pitminster is an independent community site about the church of St Andrew and St Mary, the village of Pitminster, and the families who left it for America. It is kept by descendants of one of those families, with roots in Maine, together with friends of the village.

What it is and is not

It is not the parish's official site and does not speak for the Parochial Church Council, the Blackdown Benefice or the Diocese of Bath and Wells. For services, parish business and giving, use the parish's own channels: A Church Near You and blackdown.org.uk. This site handles no money.

It tries to give the church, the village and the emigrant families equal weight, and to be useful to someone standing in the churchyard, someone who has lived here all their life, and someone in Connecticut who has just found the name Pitminster in a family Bible.

Corrections

Everything on these pages is written from the published record, listed below, and from a distance. Where the record is a tradition rather than a document, the text says so. If you know better, write to the editors; corrections are made and credited.

Privacy

The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. When you post a note on the wall or send a message, the site stores what you typed, the time, and a scrambled version of your network address used only to stop floods of spam. Email addresses are never shown and never passed to anyone else. To have a note or message removed, ask, and it will be deleted.

Add to it

Villagers' photographs, old or new, memories of the church and the pubs and the school, and any record of a Pitminster family overseas are all welcome. Write to the editors.

Friends of Pitminster is an independent community site kept by friends of the village on both sides of the Atlantic. It is not the parish's official site. For services, parish business and giving, the parish's own channels are A Church Near You and the Blackdown Benefice.
