The family wall

A place to leave a note: who you are, which family you belong to if any, where you have ended up, and what you are looking for. Descendants, villagers and visitors are all welcome. A person reads every note before it appears, so there may be a day or two's delay.

Leave a note

House rules

  • Notes are read by a person before they appear.
  • No street addresses, phone numbers or anything you would not say in the churchyard.
  • Nothing for sale.
  • If you want a note taken down, ask and it will be.
