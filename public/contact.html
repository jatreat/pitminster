Contact the editors

Corrections, additions, photographs, questions about a family, or a note you would rather not post on the wall. Messages go to the people who keep this site, not to the parish; for parish business, ring the vicarage on 01823 421757.

So the editors can reply. Never shown or shared.

What helps most

  • A photograph of the font or the east window: the site has none yet.
  • Old photographs of the village, with roughly when and where.
  • Parish-register entries or family papers that mention Pitminster families overseas.
  • Anything on these pages that is wrong.

Friends of Pitminster is an independent community site kept by friends of the village on both sides of the Atlantic. It is not the parish's official site. For services, parish business and giving, the parish's own channels are A Church Near You and the Blackdown Benefice.
